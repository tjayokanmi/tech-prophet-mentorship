How to Prepare Jollof Rice

Coconut rice is a delicious and nutritious rice dish prepared using rice, coconut milk, spices, and other ingredients. It is popular in Nigeria and other parts of the world. The coconut milk gives the rice a rich, creamy texture and a pleasant coconut flavour. Coconut rice can be served with fried plantain, chicken, fish, or vegetables.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups long-grain parboiled or Basmati rice
  • 1 large fresh coconut (or 400ml canned coconut milk)
  • 500ml blended tomatoes and Scotch bonnet peppers
  • 1 cup sliced onions
  • 1 kg to 2 kg chicken or fish (with stock reserved)
  • 2 to 3 seasoning cubes and salt to taste
  • 1 tsp curry powder and 1 tsp thyme
  • 150 ml vegetable oil or coconut oil
  • 1 heaped tbsp crayfish powder(optional)

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Wash the rice: Pour the rice into a bowl and wash thoroughly with clean water to remove excess starch. Drain the water using a sieve.
  2. Prepare the ingredients: Chop the onions, tomatoes, and bell pepper. Set them aside.
  3. Heat the oil: Place a cooking pot on medium heat and add the vegetable oil.
  4. Fry the onions Add the chopped onions and stir for about 2 minutes until they become soft.
  5. Add the spices: Add the curry powder, thyme, pepper, and seasoning cubes. Stir properly to combine the ingredients.
  6. Add the coconut milk: Pour the coconut milk and water or chicken stock into the pot. Stir and allow the mixture to boil.
  7. Add the rice: Pour the washed rice into the boiling coconut milk mixture and stir gently.
  8. Cook the rice: Cover the pot and reduce the heat to low. Allow the rice to cook for about 20–25 minutes until it becomes soft and the liquid is absorbed.
  9. Check the rice: Taste the rice to confirm that it is properly cooked. If it is still hard, add a little water and allow it to cook for a few more minutes.
  10. Allow it to rest: Turn off the heat and leave the rice covered for about 5 minutes.
  11. Serve: Stir gently with a fork or cooking spoon and serve hot with fried plantain, chicken, fish, or vegetables.

Safety Precautions and Warnings

  • Be careful when handling hot cooking pots to avoid burns. Always use oven gloves or a dry kitchen cloth.
  • Do not touch hot oil or boiling coconut milk with bare hands.
  • Always use clean water and properly washed cooking utensils when preparing food.
  • Be careful when chopping onions, peppers, and other ingredients with a sharp knife.
  • Ensure that chicken or fish added to the rice is properly cooked before serving.
  • Do not leave the cooking rice unattended for too long because it may burn.
  • Store leftover coconut rice in a covered container in the refrigerator promptly.

Cooking Terms and Their Meanings

These are some cooking terms used when preparing coconut rice:

Parboiling
This means partially boiling rice before completing the cooking process. It helps reduce the final cooking time.
Simmering
This means cooking food gently at a low temperature, with small bubbles appearing in the liquid.
